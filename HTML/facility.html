Murabaha (Asset based fianancing)

What is Murabaha?

The Murabaha is an asset-based financing model where BCS facilitates the purchase of goods or assets on behalf of a member and sells them at an agreed markup price.

Request Financing

A member requests financing for an asset (e.g., equipment, vehicles, or business inventory).

BCS Purchases Asset

BCS purchases the asset from a supplier.

BCS Sells Asset

BCS sells the asset to the member at a pre-agreed profit margin.

Repayment

The member repays in installments over a defined period.
