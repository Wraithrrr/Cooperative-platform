Qard Hasan (Cash Credit)

What is Qard Hasan?

Qard Hasan is a benevolent cash credit facility where members receive financial assistance with the understanding that the amount borrowed will be repaid without interest.

Interest-Free

Members receive financial assistance without any interest charges.

Flexible Repayment

Repayment is made within an agreed period.

Administrative Charge

A fixed administrative charge may apply.

Transparent Terms

Clear documentation of credit terms and conditions.

Please read the BCS Credit Policy Agreement Framework carefully before applying.
