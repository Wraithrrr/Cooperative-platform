Ijarah (Lease Financing)

What is Ijarah

Ijarah is a Shariah-compliant leasing arrangement where BCS purchases assets and leases them to members with flexible ownership transfer options.

Asset Leasing

Access to high-value assets without upfront ownership

Flexible Terms

Customizable lease periods and payment schedules

Clear Contracts

Transparent lease agreements with defined terms

Ownership Option

Potential ownership transfer at lease end
